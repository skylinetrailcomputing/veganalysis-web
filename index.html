Veganalysis

Vegan check for packaged foods.

Veganalysis is a barcode-scanning app that helps you spot likely non-vegan ingredients on packaged-product labels. Point your iPhone camera at a product barcode and Veganalysis returns one of three verdicts:

  • Vegan — no ingredients of animal origin detected.
  • Not vegan — one or more ingredients of animal origin detected, listed in plain language.
  • Needs review — ambiguous ingredients (like “natural flavors” or “spices”) are present that no automated system can resolve from text alone. Verify with the manufacturer.

If a product isn’t in the bundled database, Veganalysis can also scan the ingredient label directly using on-device text recognition — useful for in-store finds, imported products, and anything off the beaten path.

Privacy

Veganalysis runs on your device. No account, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately.
